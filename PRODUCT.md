# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People looking for standout trees in their area and neighbors who want to share a beautiful tree they know.

## Product Purpose

A crowdsourced neighborhood tree gallery called Local Leaf that helps people discover remarkable local trees and submit their own. It ranks photos by how much of the image Cloudinary's predominant-color analysis labels orange, red, or yellow, with a transparent rubric and approximate public location.

## Brand Personality

Warm, seasonal, curious, and community-minded. The experience should feel connected to nature and fall, welcoming both discovery and sharing.

## Anti-references

Avoid storefronts, product catalogs, sales-oriented layouts, and aggressive gamification or competition patterns.

## Design Principles

- Let the submitted trees and their seasonal colors lead.
- Make color scores understandable rather than mysterious.
- Encourage neighborhood discovery without publishing precise locations.
- Make browsing and contributing equally approachable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Ensure color is not the only means of communicating ranking, maintain readable contrast, and support keyboard and reduced-motion preferences.
